Redfield Roleplay is a roleplay gamemode for Multi Theft Auto: San Andreas (MTA:SA).

The gamemode provides a persistent roleplay environment with jobs, factions, vehicles, houses, licenses, banking, achievements, player progression and various additional gameplay systems.

## 👤 Accounts & Progression

Redfield Roleplay includes its own registration and login system with MySQL persistence.

Player data is permanently stored, including:

- Money and bank balance
- Playtime
- Level and EXP
- Jobs
- Faction membership
- Licenses
- Vehicles
- Houses
- Achievements
- Inventory and items

Players earn experience through different activities and can increase their level over time.

## 💼 Jobs

Players can select jobs and earn money through various activities across San Andreas.

Jobs can be selected through the job system and include different tasks and locations throughout the map.

The job system is integrated with the economy and player progression systems.

## 👥 Factions

Redfield Roleplay includes multiple factions with their own vehicles, headquarters, equipment and gameplay features.

- Police Department
- Reporter
- Criminal factions

Police officers can use features such as duty equipment, wanted management, arrests and surveillance systems.

Criminal factions have access to illegal activities, transports and the Gangwar system.

## 🚗 Vehicles

Players can purchase and own personal vehicles through different dealerships.

The vehicle system includes:

- Persistent vehicle ownership
- Vehicle slots
- Vehicle locking
- Vehicle respawning
- Vehicle parking
- Vehicle selling
- Fuel system
- Pay N Spray
- Vehicle tuning

Certain vehicles require the appropriate license before they can be purchased or driven.

## 🪪 Licenses

Different licenses can be acquired through the license and driving school systems.

The gamemode includes licenses for different vehicle types as well as a weapon license.

Driving tests include practical gameplay sections that must be completed before receiving the corresponding license.

## 🏠 Houses

Players can purchase houses throughout the map.

Houses provide additional features such as:

- Personal spawn points
- Vehicle parking spaces
- Lockable entrances
- House management

Available and owned houses are displayed separately on the map.

## 🏦 Banking & Economy

Redfield Roleplay features a persistent economy and banking system.

Players can create their own bank account and receive a personal PIN for accessing ATMs.

The banking system supports:

- Bank account creation
- PIN authentication
- Deposits
- Withdrawals
- Persistent bank balance

Money can be earned through jobs, factions, missions, paydays and other gameplay activities.

## 🔫 Weapons

Weapons can be purchased through Ammu-Nations after obtaining the required weapon license.

Faction members can also access faction-specific equipment depending on their faction and duty status.

Weapon purchases and licenses are integrated with the economy and permission systems.

## 🍔 Hunger

Players need to regularly take care of their hunger.

Food can be purchased from restaurants and 24/7 shops throughout the map.

The hunger system adds an additional survival element to normal roleplay gameplay.

## 📱 Phone

Players can purchase a mobile phone from 24/7 shops.

The phone provides communication features and allows players to interact with other players through the phone system.

## 🏆 Achievements & Level System

The gamemode includes a persistent achievement and progression system.

Players can earn achievements and EXP by completing different activities such as purchasing vehicles, creating a bank account and progressing through the game.

EXP is used for the level system and can also be spent through the bonus shop.

## 🎯 Missions & Activities

Redfield Roleplay includes different missions and activities beyond regular jobs.

These systems provide additional opportunities to earn money, experience and other rewards.

Faction-specific activities such as transports and Gangwars provide additional gameplay for faction members.

## 🛒 Shops

- 24/7 shops
- Ammu-Nations
- Restaurants
- Vehicle dealerships
- Tuning garages
- Pay N Sprays

## 🎮 Additional Systems

Redfield Roleplay includes several additional systems:

- Custom HUD
- Custom DX interfaces
- Custom notifications and infoboxes
- Help menu
- Administration system
- Report system
- Payday system
- Player trading
- Vehicle tuning
- Insurance system
- Hospital and respawn system
- Gangwar system
- Custom chat
- Faction chat
- Real-time synchronization
- German and English language support

## ⚙️ Installation

1. Place the Redfield Roleplay resource inside your MTA server's `resources` directory.
2. Create/import the required database tables.
3. Configure the MySQL database connection for your environment (Mysql/Mysql.lua).
5. Start the resource on your MTA server.
